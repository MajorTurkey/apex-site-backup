Apps

Two tools from Apex Field Service.

Parley

An offline meeting recorder. You record the meeting, and after you stop, it transcribes on the device. It can export a ZIP with the recording, the transcript, and the notes, and it leaves the originals untouched.

Android version 1.7.4 is the current test build. It is also in Google Play closed testing, and is not on the public Play Store yet.

$2.99/month subscription inside the app. Cancel anytime in Google Play.

Wellness Check

A field safety app. You pair with a buddy, start a timed check-in, and if you don't mark yourself okay before time is up, your buddy is alerted. There is also a panic button that shares your location and lets you call or text 911.

Android version 1.3.1. This build is in beta testing.

$2.99/month subscription inside the app. Cancel anytime in Google Play.
